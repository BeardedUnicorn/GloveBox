- Fit to me: the first time you open a lesson, hold both hands up where
  you'll practice and GloveBox sizes and centers the lesson to them, however
  far you stand from the camera. "Refit to me" on the Ready card measures
  again when you move.
- The target rings are smaller, so neighboring fingers' rings no longer
  overlap, and the rolls, roll crawl and grid slides hold the two hands a
  little farther apart. Scores are a touch stricter right at the center of a
  ring; how far out a near miss still counts is unchanged.
